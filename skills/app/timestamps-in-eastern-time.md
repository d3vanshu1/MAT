## Timestamp Display Rule

**All timestamps must be presented in Eastern Time (ET).**

- Convert UTC timestamps to ET before showing to the user
- Use ET in conversation when discussing when events occurred
- Format example: "July 20, 3:11 PM ET" (not "19:11 UTC")
- During EDT (March–November): ET = UTC − 4
- During EST (November–March): ET = UTC − 5
